# Harborline Ferries: Passenger Handbook

Harborline Ferries is a fictional operator written for this repository's test set. Nothing here describes a real company.

## Route A: Pier 4 to Gull Island

Route A sails from Pier 4 to Gull Island every hour between 06:30 and 21:30. The crossing takes 35 minutes. The ferry on this route is the MV Plover, which seats 180 passengers. Cars are not carried on Route A.

## Weather cancellations

The captain decides whether a sailing goes ahead. Sailings are cancelled when sustained wind exceeds 38 knots or when visibility drops below 400 metres. A passenger whose sailing is cancelled receives a fare credit, and the credit is valid for 90 days from the date of the cancelled sailing. Credits cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Fares and luggage

Children under 5 travel free. Children aged 5 to 15 pay half the adult fare. Bicycles are carried free of charge. Motorbikes are carried for a flat fee of 7 euros each. Passengers may bring up to two bags; a third bag costs 2 euros.

## Lost property

Anything left on board is taken to the office at Pier 4. Lost property is held for 21 days. After that period unclaimed items are donated to the Gull Island Scouts. Passengers can call the Pier 4 office between 08:00 and 17:00 to describe a lost item.

## Safety

In an emergency, passengers should walk to the muster point on the aft deck and wait for instructions from the crew. Life jackets are stored under the seats in the first row of each cabin. Children's life jackets are orange and are kept in the locker beside the aft door.
