Maple Street Library: Member Guide

Maple Street Library is a fictional branch written for this repository's test set. Nothing here describes a real library.

OPENING HOURS
The library is open Monday to Thursday from 09:00 to 20:00, on Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, and on Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. It is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

LOANS AND FINES
Books can be borrowed for 21 days and renewed once online. DVDs can be borrowed for 7 days and cannot be renewed. Reference books never leave the building. The fine for an overdue book is 15 cents per day, capped at 4 dollars per item. The fine for an overdue DVD is 50 cents per day. Members with more than 10 dollars in unpaid fines cannot borrow new items.

STUDY ROOMS
Members can reserve a study room up to 14 days in advance. A single booking lasts at most 2 hours. Rooms are held for 15 minutes after the start time and are then released to the next person on the waiting list.

3D PRINTER
The makerspace on the second floor has a 3D printer. Members must complete a 45 minute training session before they can use it. Printing costs 5 cents per gram of filament, and the maximum print size is 20 centimetres on each side.

MEMBERSHIP
Membership is free for residents of the Maple district. Out-of-district members pay 25 dollars per year. A library card replacement costs 3 dollars.
